## Reading a session code

- A code is a track prefix plus three digits, for example BOA208 (BOA was the Build on AWS track).
- The first digit is the level: 100s are introductory overviews, 200s go deeper, 300s are advanced, 400s are expert. The 2026 official session page also lists 500 as the top level.
- Codes ending in -R are repeats of a popular session, often at another venue.
- Search the catalog by track prefix to find related sessions.

## Choosing levels

Lower-level sessions can still help experienced people, for example to learn how to explain a topic simply. Trying a level above your usual one can spark new ideas. Nobody checks your level at the door.

## If you can't get a seat (2023)

- Breakout sessions were recorded and posted to the AWS Events YouTube channel.
- Look for -R repeats, overflow rooms and talks in the content hubs at each venue.
- PeerTalk let some speakers offer one-to-one meetings (2023).
