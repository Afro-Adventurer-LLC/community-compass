## All Builders Welcome: A newcomer's first AWS re:Invent

This is personal experience from an All Builders Welcome Grant recipient, published December 13, 2023. It is not official AWS guidance. Eligibility rules, dates, and benefits for later years are governed by the current official Terms and Conditions.

This is the story of my adventure as a recipient of an All Builders Welcome Grant from AWS, and my first-ever AWS re:Invent.

### Navigating re:Invent as an All Builders Welcome Grant winner

I found myself in this fortunate position when I won an All Builders Welcome (ABW) Grant from AWS. This grant, which provides a complimentary trip to re:Invent, acknowledged my individual accomplishments and is also part of a bigger AWS initiative to bridge the gap in tech for people from underrepresented communities.

### My daily experience

We kicked off the week on Sunday evening with a fun welcome reception. We were shuttled to Area 15, an indoor, futuristic fun zone, where we were greeted with food, music, games, and free entry into several of the Area 15 attractions. It was a fun and low-pressure way for all of us grant winners to break the ice and shake off our nerves.

During the rest of re:Invent, there was a dedicated lounge for ABW grant winners. This was a quiet place where we were welcome to go to take a break from the hustle and bustle, meet with our mentors, eat delicious snacks, and share our ongoing experiences with the other grant winners. And as an added bonus, AWS offered all grant recipients the opportunity to get free professional headshots taken.

On Thursday evening, AWS threw the ABW grantees a lovely closing reception. After drinks and some fancy hors d'oeuvres, we all packed together for hilarious group photos.

### How to apply for the AWS All Builders Welcome Grant (historical, 2023)

For anyone interested in applying for the ABW grant, the qualifications described at the time of this post were:

- Be an underrepresented technologist in the first five years of your career
- Have a strong interest in cloud computing
- Be able to commit to attending all four days of re:Invent
- Be at least 21 years old
- Have not previously accepted a grant to an AWS event
- Not be an employee, intern, independent contractor, officer, director, legal or financial advisor of AWS or any of its parents, subsidiaries, affiliates, advisors, or advertising/promotion agencies

The application for the grant opens up in the summer and will be shared on the AWS re:Invent site when it opens. So keep an eye out next June or July for it to open back up again.

### The dilemma: Network or attend breakout sessions and workshops?

Coming to re:Invent, I faced a common dilemma among attendees: the choice between networking or immersing myself in the wealth of educational sessions and workshops that were available. Travel time between venues and sessions could take anywhere from 20 minutes to an hour.

### The FOMO factor

AWS re:Invent is more than just a conference; it's a thriving community where connections are made, collaborations are built, and friendships flourish. A lot of attendees, including many of my fellow ABW grant winners, spent quite a bit more time than I did networking and building community. I don't regret choosing sessions, but I'll admit it's a bittersweet feeling.
