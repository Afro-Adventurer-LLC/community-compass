## About this post

Personal recap from Amelia Hough-Ross, a new AWS Community Builder at the time, published December 8, 2023 on DEV Community (AWS Community Builders). Venues and events are from 2023; several (such as Mandalay Bay) are not 2026 venues. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Travel and arrival

- A direct flight is worth it; connections caused the author missed flights and delays.
- Airport badge pickup was quick (2023).
- Expect a long travel day and plan to rest after arriving.

## Sessions

- Workshops were not recorded (2023), so they are worth attending live.
- Popular sessions fill early; the author arrived to a 6:30 AM yoga session with the doors already closed.
- Keynotes can be streamed from your hotel room.
- Friday sessions can be worth staying for if your flight allows.
- Plan the next day's schedule the night before, since many sessions overlap.

## Community and networking (2023)

- AWS Community Builders ran a mixer, a women's mixer and a card game, and some swag was only given out at community events.
- Networking lunches after panels led to real professional connections.
- GameDay team challenges were a good way to meet people.
- The Expo is a good place to talk with vendors.

## Rest

Don't overbook; you will often have to choose between events. The Thursday party ran short, which helped people make early Friday sessions (2023).
