## About this post

Personal experience from Masoom Tulsiani, an AWS Community Builder and freelance cloud architect, who attended re:Invent 2024 through the All Builders Welcome grant and was later selected as an ABW alumni advisor for 2025. Published July 5, 2025. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## What the grant included for the author (2024)

As the author listed it: a full conference pass, flights to Las Vegas, five hotel nights, mentoring sessions, reserved keynote seating, grantee-only events, swag, and an Uber voucher for airport transfers. Current benefits are set by the official ABW Terms and the grant team.

## Session planning (2024)

- Favorite sessions early and reserve the moment reservations open. The author saw 300–400 level chalk talks fill in about a minute.
- Breakouts were on YouTube within hours, so the author put chalk talks first.

## Logistics (2024)

- The Venetian, MGM Grand and Mandalay Bay were all used; the bus between The Venetian and MGM or Mandalay Bay took about 30–40 minutes, so avoid both ends of the Strip on the same day.
- Arrive at least a day early. The author's flight was cancelled, they arrived very late and missed the ABW opening event.
- Bring your badge to vendor events.

## Vendor and evening events (2024)

Register for vendor parties with a work email and bring your badge. The author's examples included outings sponsored by vendors such as Topgolf and a helicopter ride.
