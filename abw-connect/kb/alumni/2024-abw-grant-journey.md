## About this post

Personal experience from Joseph Arriola, an AWS Community Builder, published December 26, 2024 about re:Invent 2024, which he attended on the All Builders Welcome grant. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## What stood out

- The author describes the grant as covering travel and giving access to grantee workshops, sessions and activities, mentoring and networking events (2024).
- He met other grant recipients and industry leaders from many countries.
- Keynotes and workshops were the core of the week; the Expo showed work from startups and large companies.
- Most of the post is a recap of 2024 launch announcements rather than attendee tips.
