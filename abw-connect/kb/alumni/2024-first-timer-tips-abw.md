## About this post

Personal experience from Dale Orders, an AWS Community Builder and user group leader, who attended re:Invent 2024 for the first time through the All Builders Welcome grant. Published January 6, 2025. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Tips

- Pack light and keep about half your suitcase free for swag; some attendees needed a second bag to get it all home.
- Book sessions as soon as you can. Walk-up lines often formed 30 minutes or more before start, and the author missed sessions that way.
- Apply early and widely for evening social events. Most fill up and many applications get turned down.
- Plan your days ahead of time and manage the schedule in the AWS Events app.
- Limit each day to one or two venues. In 2024 sessions were spread over five hotels along about 4.2 miles (6.8 km) of the Strip, and moving between them, including finding the room, could take an hour or more. If you use two venues in a day, pick ones close together.
- Do less. You can't do everything, so prioritize, rest, and feel free to say no to invitations. Many sessions are recorded.
- Meet people. The author found networking was the highlight; even non-technical conversations are worth starting.
