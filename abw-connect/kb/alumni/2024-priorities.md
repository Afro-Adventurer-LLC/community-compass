## About this post

Personal view from Dana Batorova, an AWS Community Builder, on AWS Builder Center, published January 4, 2025 about re:Invent 2024. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Priorities to balance

- Networking during the day: meet mentors, possible collaborators and tech leads at meetups, peer talks, chalk talks and jams, and talk to AWS employees.
- Networking after hours: parties and evening events are a relaxed setting for conversations that can turn into real connections.
- Technical learning: plan around keynotes and breakout sessions tied to your goals, take notes, and rewatch recordings later.
- Hands-on practice: workshops, labs, bootcamps and jams let you try services in a guided setting.
- Expo: see what AWS partners build, how they use AWS and what trends are emerging, and collect swag.
- Try something new: go to at least one session or activity outside your usual area.
