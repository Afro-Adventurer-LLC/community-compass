## About this guide

Guide by Maria-Alexandra Radu, an AWS Community Builder and past ABW grant recipient, published June 8, 2025. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Before applying

Eligibility rules are in the official ABW Terms. Two practical points she adds: international applicants arrange their own visa, and no certifications or prior AWS experience are needed.

## What the grant included in her experience

A full conference pass, round-trip airfare, a private hotel room for about five days and four nights, an Uber card for airport transfers, a gift card for incidentals, and cohort swag such as a backpack. Benefits can change by year; the official Terms and the grant team are the source for 2026.

## ABW programming she describes

Reserved front-row seating at keynotes and meetups, a welcome event, mentoring with AWS experts and leaders, and a Slack channel for grantees and program coordinators.

## Application advice

- Tell your own story; reviewers are people looking for genuine motivation, so avoid generic or AI-written answers.
- Show your interest in cloud through actions: talks, side projects, courses, certifications, hackathons, clubs and self-study all count.
- Be clear about career goals and what you want from the conference.
- Explain how you help others and how you will share what you learn.
- Describe barriers you have overcome.
- Keep each answer to two or three main points; she suggests the STAR method.
- Many grantees applied more than once, so keep trying.
