## About this post

Personal reflection from Aadrit Aggarwal, a graduate student who attended re:Invent 2025 for the first time on the All Builders Welcome grant. Published December 31, 2025. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Lessons

- Plan early. The author started about two weeks ahead, mapping tracks, companies and technologies to look for.
- Leave buffer time. Unplanned hallway conversations often turned out more valuable than the scheduled sessions.
- Expo conversations with product managers and solutions architects were technical and practical, not sales pitches.
- Gamified activities such as /dev/quest in the Developers Pavilion (2025) rewarded visiting several booths and made learning more fun.
- Walking between venues takes real time; the author had to rush from The Venetian to Wynn for a chalk talk.
- Stay open to unplanned opportunities. One conversation with an AWS Professional Services director became an ongoing mentorship, and the author recommends keeping in touch beyond a LinkedIn connection.
