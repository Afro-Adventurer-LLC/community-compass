## About this post

Personal reflection from Suzana Melo, a full-stack engineer and AWS Community Builder, published December 8, 2025 on her own site. She was an ABW grantee at re:Invent 2024 and returned in 2025 as an alumni advisor guiding a new cohort. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Takeaways

- re:Invent has games, workshops, meetups, breakout sessions and a lot of chances to network, including with mentors, collaborators, future teammates and employers.
- Join community groups for peer support before and after the event.
- Career habits she recommends: seek more than one mentor, study in short consistent blocks (she uses the Pomodoro technique), practice what you learn and share it through posts, talks or projects, teach others, and compare yourself only with your past self.
