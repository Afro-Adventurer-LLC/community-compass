## About this post

Personal guide from Gleison Pires, a DevOps engineer and AWS user group leader who attended re:Invent 2025 on the All Builders Welcome grant. Published January 2, 2026. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Know your style

The author sees three kinds of attendee, and most people mix them: the Networker (meetups, breakfasts, side events), the Collector (swag and booth prizes) and the Explorer (sightseeing and downtime in Las Vegas). Knowing which you are helps you plan.

## Planning

- Plan the week in a tool like Notion and reserve your must-see sessions early (in 2025 reservations opened about two weeks before the event).
- Don't overbook. The author filled every slot, then cut sessions to make room for breaks, networking and swag.
- The week starts Sunday with badge pickup and settling in.
- Wrapping up sessions around 4–5 PM leaves evenings for side events or seeing the Strip.
- conferenceparties.com lists unofficial happy hours and parties.

## Health and comfort

Eat regularly and drink water, since it is easy to skip meals. The desert air is very dry: bring lip balm and moisturizer, and nosebleeds can happen.

## Swag and packing

Plan how swag gets home before you collect it. The author brought a backpack, a carry-on and an empty checked bag and still struggled to fit everything.

## Capturing ideas

Write down ideas from talks right away, as notes or quick sketches, or by explaining them to someone.
