## My first time experiencing AWS re:Invent, thanks to the ABW grant!

This is personal experience from an All Builders Welcome Grant recipient at re:Invent 2025. It is not official AWS guidance.

re:Invent 2025 was an incredible experience and could not have been possible without the AWS All Builders Welcome Grant. I was selected alongside many others globally to attend the event all expenses paid.

If you are in the first 5 years of your career and meet the requirements for the application criteria — I urge you to apply for the grant in 2026 when they open next year. The opportunity to attend through the grant is once in a lifetime.

### Day 0: Arriving to Las Vegas and the ABW Grant Welcome Reception

After 16 hours of travelling from Belfast, I arrived at Harry Reid airport in Las Vegas around 5pm on the 30th November, the day before re:Invent officially kicked off. AWS staff checked us in to collect our badges, which was used as a pass into re:Invent hosting hotels, sessions, and even some exclusive parties that happened in the evenings throughout the week.

We got a Lyft over to Treasure Island, checked in, then headed over to the Welcome Reception for the ABW Grant via shuttle bus, which was a 7pm start at a venue within Area15. This was a chance for all of the grantees to connect with each other and the alumni advisors, play games, have a couple of drinks, eat some food, and overall break the ice before heading into the first day of re:Invent.

### Day 1: Sessions, House of Kiro, and the Expo Hall

My first session was scheduled for 8am at MGM Grand. Before heading to re:Invent, I was aware it's large scale across the strip and had many people sharing tips about leaving enough time to get to your next session. Good time management and discipline are definitely needed to ensure you attend all of the sessions you want to see throughout the week, but it is also important to take the conference at a pace that suits you.

The expo hall opened at 4pm. The conversations, swag, and networking that can happen with companies in this hall is an experience I had only ever read or heard about.

### ABW-specific programming I experienced

ABW grantees got priority access to all of the keynotes. They fill up fast, so it is great to know that as long as you show up within the time window you need to be there for — you will have a seat reserved if you are a part of the grant.

I started one day with the speed mentoring and networking session that was created specifically for participants of the ABW Grant. Before another session, I hung out in the ABW Lounge to get some professional headshots.

As a 2025 recipient of the grant, I received 100% off one certification of my choice and as a ticket holder for re:Invent, I have another 50% off discount that I can also use.

On the final evening there was a closing reception in the Wynn for the All Builders Welcome Grant before re:Play, the official closing party for the main conference week.

### Takeaways from a first-time grantee

- re:Invent is HUGE and sees around 60,000 attendees from all around the world and spans across 6 hotels on the Las Vegas strip — your step count will be insane.
- Your network will grow rapidly. I met so many people through the ABW program, during sessions, and even just when travelling between venues.
- You will learn a lot about yourself in an environment with many like-minded people who are willing to give advice over a session or a coffee.
- Pace yourself. The tiredness hit me the most on Day 3 and it is very important to take breaks.
