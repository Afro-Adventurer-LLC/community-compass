## About this guide

Guide by Livio Arleo, an AWS employee, posted on AWS Builder Center on November 19, 2025, for re:Invent 2025 (December 1–5, 2025). Venue and transport details are for 2025. re:Invent 2026 runs November 30 to December 4, 2026 per the official pages. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Badge and registration (2025)

- You must be registered and wear your badge and lanyard in event spaces; attendees must be at least 18 on pickup day.
- Upload an attendee photo in the registration portal ahead of time, or staff take one at pickup. Bring government-issued photo ID.
- 2025 pickup was at the airport and at Mandalay Bay, MGM Grand and The Venetian.
- Showing your Builder Center QR code got limited-edition swag, Builders Loft access and entry to challenges such as /dev/quest (2025).

## Planning your schedule

- Favorite sessions in the catalog, but favoriting does not hold a seat.
- Reserved-seat holders need to be there 10 minutes before start, after which unclaimed seats go to the walk-up line.
- The guide says session queues open about an hour before a session.
- Plan breaks: quiet time, short walks, proper meals.

## Packing

ID, devices and chargers; layers for indoor and outdoor temperature swings; a spare bag or empty suitcase space for swag; a second pair of comfortable, already broken-in shoes; lip balm, lotion and a water bottle for the dry air; small snacks.

## Getting around (2025)

- Allow 30–45 minutes between activities at different venues.
- Encore connects directly to Wynn, and a bridge links Wynn and The Venetian.
- In 2025 shuttles ran between most venues but not between Wynn and The Venetian or between Caesars Forum and The Venetian (those were walking routes). The app showed live shuttle times.
- The Las Vegas Monorail was free with a badge (stations used: MGM, Horseshoe/Paris, Flamingo/Caesars Palace, Harrah's/The LINQ).
- Rideshare and taxi pickup zones are at the venues and hotels. The author advises against renting a car.
- re:Play 2025 was at the Las Vegas Festival Grounds. For 2026 the official agenda lists Allegiant Stadium.
