## About this guide

Guide by Abhishek Joshi, an ABW alumni advisor and AWS Community Builder who received the grant for re:Invent 2025. Published June 17, 2026 for 2026 applicants. Program details describe his 2025 experience. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## What the author says the grant covers

Full conference pass; round-trip airfare booked through the AWS designated travel agency; five hotel nights on the re:Invent campus; one certification exam voucher (he says up to $300); a $250 gift card for incidentals; and a $75 Uber/Lyft voucher for airport transfers. He notes personal expenses beyond these are not covered and return flights must be within 14 days of the last event day. The gift card, exam voucher and ride voucher are his account, not the official Terms; confirm with the grant team.

## ABW programming the author describes (2025)

- Reserved front seating at keynotes and major sessions.
- A dedicated ABW track of roughly 12–13 career sessions, including a STAR-method interview workshop, a personal portfolio website workshop, and talks on imposter syndrome and tackling unfamiliar problems. He found this track the most valuable part.
- Built-in time with AWS experts and leaders as mentors.
- A welcome reception for grantees.
- A private Slack channel for the cohort before the event.
- A welcome kit with cohort swag such as a backpack.

## Practical advice for grantees

- Check your calendar for re:Invent week before applying; the author says deferral is generally not allowed (the official Terms say AWS may allow one deferral case by case).
- Budget your own spending money for personal costs.
- After the event, apply what you learned, keep in touch with new connections and set goals for the next year.

## Application timing and tips (2026 cycle)

- 2026 applications opened in late June 2026 and closed July 14, 2026; decisions have historically come around September, and the acceptance email is the official confirmation.
- Write answers yourself; AI can help brainstorm but should not write them.
- Be concrete with numbers, project names and outcomes, and tie the request to a plan for what you will do afterwards.
- Show what you did with limited resources and informal community help, and pick the eligibility angle that is true for you.
- Don't submit in the last 48 hours; revise after a night's rest.
