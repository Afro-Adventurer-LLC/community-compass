## About this guide

Planning guide by Payoj Mistry, an AWS employee, posted on AWS Builder Center on November 12, 2026 (updated November 13, 2026). It is a community planning guide, not an official re:Invent page. The author says session details were checked against the event catalog on November 11, 2026. It is not official AWS guidance. Dates, venues, benefits and rules for re:Invent 2026 come from the official re:Invent pages and the ABW Terms and Conditions, which win if anything here differs.

## Campus clusters

- North: The Venetian/Palazzo (with the Expo) and Wynn/Encore. Keynotes, the Expo, the Builder Zone, and the Thursday security focus day at Wynn are here.
- Center: Caesars Forum and Caesars Palace. Caesars Forum has the Customer Showcase, the Certification Lounge and the Academy rooms.
- South: MGM Grand, where many deep AI and agent workshops run.

## Walking and shuttle time estimates

- Venetian to Wynn/Encore: about 10–15 minutes on foot.
- Venetian to Caesars Forum: about 15–20 minutes on foot.
- Caesars Forum to Caesars Palace: about 5–10 minutes (roughly 0.4 miles, separate buildings).
- Center cluster to MGM Grand: about 20–25 minutes by shuttle.
- North cluster to MGM Grand: 30 minutes or more by shuttle.
- Shuttles start around 6 AM and run until about 9:15 PM Monday, 8:30 PM Tuesday and Wednesday, and 5:45 PM Thursday. re:Play shuttles start at 6:15 PM Thursday. Lines are longest right after keynotes and at lunch.

## How to move around

- Keep each half day inside one cluster and change clusters over lunch.
- Allow at least 45 minutes for a north to south trip.
- Wear comfortable shoes; people report walking more than 10 miles a day.

## Keynotes

The guide lists all keynotes at The Venetian, Ballroom E, also streamed: developer keynote Monday 7:30–8:45 PM; CEO keynote Tuesday 8:30–10:30 AM; data and AI keynote Wednesday 8:30–10:00 AM; partner keynote Wednesday 3:00–4:00 PM; technology innovation keynote Thursday 8:30–10:00 AM. Keynotes are walk-up, so arrive 15–20 minutes early. If a keynote clashes with a workshop you reserved, the guide suggests going to the workshop and watching the keynote replay.

## Meals

Breakfast and lunch are served at the venues Monday through Thursday. Friday service is limited, at The Venetian and Wynn/Encore until about 1 PM. Eat near your next session and use lunch to switch clusters.

## Expo

- Welcome reception in the Expo: Monday 4–7 PM. Open Tuesday and Wednesday 10 AM–6 PM and Thursday 10 AM–4 PM. Expo happy hour Wednesday 4:30–6 PM.
- Stops the guide recommends: AWS Village kiosks, the AWS for Industries Zone (booth 111), the Partner Advantage Zone, the AWS Marketplace booth and the Builder Zone.
- Visit sponsor booths Tuesday morning when stock is full and lines are short, and use Thursday 10 AM–2 PM for a last pass. Bring an empty tote bag.

## Badge, swag and certification

- Pick up your badge and Sunday swag early. The guide names The Venetian, MGM Grand, Caesars Forum and the airport (T1 or T3); the official agenda lists badge pickup at the airport, MGM Grand and The Venetian, so check the agenda.
- The Certification Lounge is in Caesars Forum, Academy 407, Monday to Thursday 7:30 AM–5 PM. The Golden Jacket Celebration for active AWS certification holders is Wednesday 5:30–7 PM in the same room.
- In-person attendees get a 50% exam discount code in the attendee portal; the exam must be taken by January 31, 2027.

## Evenings and side events

- Networking receptions Tuesday 6–8 PM (Caesars Palace or The Venetian) and Wednesday 6–8 PM (Caesars Palace, The Venetian or Wynn/Encore).
- AMPED industries and startup party Wednesday from 7 PM at AREA15; RSVP required.
- re:Play Thursday 6:30–10:30 PM at Allegiant Stadium.
- conferenceparties.com lists many sponsor parties, most invite-only; request invites early and confirm with the host. Community extras mentioned: a Sunday hackathon, Tuesday trivia and game night, a Wednesday fun run and a LEGO build.

## Planning tips

- Session picks by career stage: early career, hands-on workshops; mid career, 300–400 level deep dives; senior staff, keynotes and strategy sessions.
- An AWS Events MCP server (api.awsevents.com/mcp, sign in with Builder ID) can read your schedule and suggest an itinerary that includes travel time.
- Times are Pacific. The guide notes that early December is on PST (UTC−8) even though the official agenda labels times PDT.
- The series also has posts by role, by industry, and for the AI, security and data tracks.
