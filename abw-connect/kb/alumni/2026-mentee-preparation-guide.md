## About this guide

This is a mentor-prepared checklist for All Builders Welcome mentees attending AWS re:Invent 2026. It is practical community guidance, not official AWS policy. Benefits, voucher amounts, deadlines, venue locations, schedules, transport arrangements, and program requirements are time-sensitive and must be confirmed in current messages from the ABW team or official AWS re:Invent sources.

## Confirm travel details

- Confirm re:Invent registration and upload a badge photo in the re:Invent portal.
- Confirm flight and hotel bookings.
- If a visa is still pending, update the grant team as soon as possible and confirm the applicable deadline with them.
- Plan where and when to collect the event badge. The guide suggests Sunday is typically quieter.
- Carry valid identification, a passport, and a visa when required.
- Consider travel insurance and check whether credit cards charge international fees.

## Set up essential apps

- Download the AWS Events, Slack, and Lyft apps.
- Use the AWS Events app to build an agenda and add all grant events.
- Check the ABW Slack channel regularly for program updates.
- The guide says a Lyft voucher may be distributed by the ABW team in mid-November. The document mentions a value of $75 USD, but recipients should confirm the current amount, validity, and instructions with the ABW team.
- Las Vegas airport generally provides Wi-Fi, which may help attendees book rides without a local SIM.
- Check whether another ABW attendee arrives at a similar time before arranging shared transport.

## Pack and pace yourself

- Bring comfortable, supportive shoes because the event involves substantial walking.
- Pack layers because indoor and outdoor temperatures vary.
- Bring a portable charger for event apps, networking, and notes.
- Carry water and lip balm for the dry Las Vegas climate.
- Leave luggage space for items collected at the event.
- Pace the week, take breaks, and decline optional invitations when rest is needed.

## Build an agenda

- Use the AWS Events app and official event catalog as the primary schedule sources.
- Add ABW sessions first, then reserve other sessions.
- In the event catalog, use **Reserve a Seat** when reservations are available.
- For walk-up sessions or full sessions, the guide recommends arriving about 30 minutes early in case seats become available.
- Favorite sessions of interest and show favorites in the calendar.

### Session-planning suggestions

- Prioritize chalk talks, workshops, and builder sessions because the guide says these are generally not recorded. Confirm recording availability for the current event.
- Schedule sessions in the same venue where possible.
- Choose a suitable technical level: 100 beginner, 200 intermediate, 300 advanced, 400 expert, and 500 deep dive.
- Limit each day to one or two venues and approximately three or four sessions to reduce travel and fatigue.
- Mix formats such as breakout sessions, chalk talks, builder sessions, workshops, theater sessions, and lightning sessions.
- Leave generous travel time between venues. The guide warns that a reserved seat may be released to walk-in attendees if the holder is not present at least 15 minutes before a session.

## Attend ABW activities

- Add all ABW activities to the agenda and confirm their current times and locations in official program communications.
- Check Slack and email for changes.
- The guide references Sunday, November 29, 2026 kickoff activities at Caesars Forum and the Venetian, but the extracted document does not include the underlying activity list. Confirm these details with the ABW team.

## Plan social and community activities

- Consider community meetups, lounges, and networking events in addition to technical sessions.
- Register early for optional events because attendance may be limited.
- Monitor email for invitations, cancellations, and schedule changes.
- Arrange meeting times and locations in advance because meeting space can be limited.
- The guide mentions ABW, Certification, Developer, and Community Builder lounges.
- It identifies an ABW grant lounge on level 1 of the Venetian in room 501. Confirm the current location before travelling to it.
- The guide links to `https://conferenceparties.com/reinvent2026/`, which is a third-party site rather than an official AWS source.

## Visit the expo thoughtfully

- The expo can be useful for learning about AWS partners and products as well as collecting promotional items.
- Arrive earlier in the week if particular items are important, but take only items that will be used.
- Leave luggage capacity for anything collected.
- Confirm current expo opening times in the AWS Events app; the source document's extracted schedule is incomplete.

## Navigate venues

- Review current event maps in the AWS Events app.
- Distances along the Las Vegas Strip are longer than they may appear on a map.
- The guide groups Encore, Wynn, the Venetian, and Caesars Forum toward one part of the event campus, with MGM Grand farther south.
- It describes Treasure Island as being across from the Venetian, but attendees should confirm their own assigned hotel and current walking routes.
- Signs and event staff can help with directions inside venues.

## Understand transport options

- The guide says re:Invent provides shuttle buses between most event venues. Confirm current routes and times in the AWS Events app.
- It says walking connections serve Wynn and the Venetian, Caesars Forum and the Venetian, and Encore and Wynn.
- It describes the Las Vegas Monorail as free for re:Invent badge holders from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. during the event, with trains every four to eight minutes and relevant stops including Harrah's/The LINQ, MGM, Horseshoe/Paris, and Flamingo/Caesars Palace. These operating details and eligibility must be verified for 2026 at `https://www.lvmonorail.com/` or through official event transport guidance.

## Other preparation

- Check the expiration date of any AWS exam voucher and complete the exam before it expires.
- Attend mentor-group virtual meetings and contact the assigned mentor with questions.
- Budget for customary US service tips. The guide suggests 10–20%, depending on the service.

## After re:Invent

- The guide says participants may be moved to an ABW Alumni Slack channel after the event.
- Complete the post-event ABW survey when it is sent.
- Consider the AWS Community Builders program at `https://builder.aws.com/community/community-builders`.
- The guide says the program may offer AWS credits, exam vouchers, conference discounts, training, community opportunities, and other benefits. Confirm current benefits and application requirements on the official program page.
- The guide says interested alumni may be able to apply as Alumni Advisors in a later cycle. Confirm timing in official ABW communications.
