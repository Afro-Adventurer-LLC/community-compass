## Accessibility at re:Invent

re:Invent is built to work for everyone. Here's what's available, what you can request, and how to get support before and during the event.

### Services available to all attendees

#### Accessible seating

Accessible seating is available in keynotes and session venues for attendees who are blind or low vision, deaf or hard-of-hearing, or who use mobility devices. Accessibility icons in and near the venue will guide you to these seats. For workshops, code talks, and sessions, an ambassador will be available in the room to assist with seating.

#### All-gender restrooms

All-gender restrooms will be available throughout the event.

#### Assistive listening devices

Assistive listening devices will be available for all keynotes, breakout sessions, lightning talks, and chalk talks.

#### Lactation room

A private, secure space will be provided for lactating attendees to express milk. A fridge will be available in the space.

#### Live captioning

All keynotes, breakout sessions, and lightning talks will feature live captioning for session content. For other sessions, iPads are available to display AI-powered live captions at your seat. The iPads will be provided on a first-come, first-served basis.

#### Reflection room

A quiet space that attendees can use for meditation, prayer, yoga, or finding resources for sensory needs will be available on campus.

#### Sensory notice

Some areas of re:Invent, especially the Expo and keynotes, include loud noise, moving lights, and atmospheric fog. Earplugs, fidget tools, noise-cancelling headphones, strobe-reducing glasses, and visual cue and feeling thermometer cards are available at the reflection room.

#### Translations for keynotes

Live translations in Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, and Spanish are available for keynotes. Headsets are provided at each venue. Additional languages are available via livestream.

### Services available by request

#### How to request a service

The following services are only available by advance request. Contact customer support (awsreinvent-support@amazon.com) to coordinate. The deadline for all requests is Nov. 13, 2026. Requests received after this date cannot be guaranteed.

#### CART (Communication Access Realtime Translation)

CART services, which provide real-time text transcription of spoken content, are available by request. Include this in your request by November 13, 2026.

#### Accessible transportation

Accessible transportation across the re:Invent campus will be available throughout the week, including for re:Play, the official AWS re:Invent party. This service does not include transportation to or from the airport.

Contact the accessible transportation concierge at least one hour prior to requested pickup. Wait times may vary. Requests will be accepted starting on Sunday, Nov. 29. The concierge phone number will be available at a later date.

#### American Sign Language (ASL) interpretation

ASL interpretation is available for sessions, keynotes, and events.

#### Dietary needs

Halal and Kosher meals are available. Gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, and common allergy accommodations are included as part of the standard buffet.

#### Mobility devices

Pickup details will be shared closer to the event. If you didn't request one in advance, visit the help desk on-site. All devices must be returned to the hotel bell desk at checkout.

#### Sighted guide

A sighted guide service is available by request for attendees who are blind or low vision.

### More information

#### Service animals

Service animals are welcome at all re:Invent venues. Designated relief areas will be available. Contact your hotel directly to confirm their service animal policy.

#### Support during re:Invent

An accessibility concierge will be available by phone or text during event hours to assist with accessibility questions or needs. The phone number will be made available closer to the event. You can also email customer support (awsreinvent-support@amazon.com) or visit a help desk on-site.
