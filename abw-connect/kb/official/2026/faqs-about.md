## About AWS re:Invent

### What is AWS re:Invent?

AWS re:Invent is five days in Las Vegas with 2,200+ sessions. You'll find workshops, hands-on labs, chalk talks, demos, technical deep dives, and keynotes covering the full range of AWS services and AI. Get direct access to AWS experts and engineers, go hands-on with new launches the day they're announced, and connect with cross-industry practitioners solving problems like yours. The expo features 450+ AWS Partners across every industry. You'll leave with answers you came for and connections you didn't expect.

### What are the top five reasons to attend AWS re:Invent?

94% of past re:Invent attendees recommend it to a colleague.

1. 2,200+ sessions in every format from workshops to demos, covering the full range of AWS services. You'll find honest, technical depth from AWS experts who help you understand how services work, where they fit your architecture, and how to put it into practice.
2. Executive and strategic programming designed for leaders making platform, investment, and organizational decisions - with direct access to AWS leadership and peers navigating similar challenges.
3. Direct access to AWS Partners in the expo who can help you scale, implement, and integrate. Plus, Ask the Experts sessions where you sit down with AWS engineers on your specific challenges.
4. Real stories from customers doing the work. Past speakers have included leaders from Adobe, Anthropic, JPMorgan Chase, Sony, WRITER, and more — sharing what worked, what didn't, and where they're headed.
5. Community meetups, social events, and networking with peers and leaders across the cloud and AI community worldwide.

### Who is AWS re:Invent for?

re:Invent is for technical practitioners, security professionals, and business decision-makers across every industry and experience level. Sessions range from 100-level foundational to 500-level deep dives, and expo, labs, and workshops are designed for anyone who wants to go hands-on. 47% of attendees in 2025 were first-timers.

### What does a typical day at re:Invent look like?

Here's what a day at re:Invent looks like: a keynote in the morning, followed by a chalk talk where architects diagram solutions in real time and a hands-on workshop completed in a small group around a table. Walk the expo to meet AWS Partners relevant to your industry, then sit down one-on-one with an AWS engineer in an Ask the Experts session. In the evening, join a focused meetup with practitioners in your space.

### When and where is AWS re:Invent 2026?

Nov. 30 – Dec. 4 in Las Vegas, NV. The conference takes place across multiple venues including Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, Encore, MGM Grand, The Venetian, and Wynn.

### Can I watch re:Invent online?

Yes. Keynotes and select live programming will be livestreamed for free. Registration for livestream will open later this year.
