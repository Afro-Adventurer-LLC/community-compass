## Accessibility Services

### What accessibility services will be available at re:Invent?

We want re:Invent to be accessible for everyone. The following services will be available at the conference for all attendees with no advance request required:

- Assistive listening
- Translations for keynotes (Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, and LATAM Spanish)
- English captions for all content spaces
- Dispersed and designated seating
- Lactation rooms
- Reflection rooms
- All-gender restrooms

These additional services can be requested in advance:

- ASL (American Sign Language) interpretation
- Sighted guide
- Mobility device rental
- Kosher meals
- Halal meals
- Accessible transportation

To request any of these services or inquire about a service you don't see listed, including neurodivergent and sensory need requests, contact customer support at awsreinvent-support@amazon.com. To properly coordinate services, all requests must be received by November 14th. Requests for services received after this date cannot be guaranteed. Please visit our accessibility services page for more information.

### Are you offering accessible transportation?

Yes. Accessible transportation will be available during transportation hours. Attendees can make arrangements at least one hour prior to their pickup. The phone number will be available prior to the event. Please visit our accessibility services page for more information.
