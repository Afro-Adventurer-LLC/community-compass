## At the Conference

### What meals are served during re:Invent?

Breakfast, lunch, and snacks will be served on full conference days. All dishes will be labeled with the following allergens/ingredients: dairy, gluten, tree nuts, shellfish, eggs, peanuts, soy, alcohol, vegetarian, and vegan.

### Will kosher and halal meals be available?

Yes. Halal and kosher meals will be available while supplies last. Please contact customer support (awsreinvent-support@amazon.com) to pre-order your meals.

### How can I connect with other attendees at re:Invent?

Focused meetups organized by role and interest, professional networking receptions, and community activities. Connections here tend to start around shared technical problems rather than business cards. Many attendees describe hallway conversations that became ongoing partnerships. Additional details will be added to the event catalog in the coming months.

## Mobile App

### Where can I download the AWS Events app?

The AWS Events app is now available and free on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Search "AWS Events" to find it. We recommend downloading or updating before you arrive so your schedule, maps, and event information are ready on day one.

### How do I sign in to the AWS Events app this year?

The AWS Events app now uses AWS Builder ID for sign-in. If you already have a Builder ID from other AWS services, you can use it to log in directly. If not, you'll be prompted to create one when you open the app. Builder ID gives you a single identity across the re:Invent app and web experience.

### What can I do in the app?

The app is your planning tool before the event and your guide while you're there. Get AI-powered session recommendations based on your role and interests, build and manage your personal schedule, reserve seats, and sync everything to Outlook, Google Calendar, or your phone's native calendar. On-site, use interactive maps and turn-by-turn wayfinding to get between venues, and stay up to date on schedule changes and event updates through real-time notifications.

### Can I reserve seats for sessions in the app?

Yes. When reserved seating opens, you can browse available sessions and reserve your seat directly in the app. If your plans change, release a reservation from your schedule and pick a different session. Reserved seating availability depends on session capacity and opens at the same time in both the app and on the web.

### What is the AWS re:Invent code of conduct?

We firmly believe in the value and importance of an environment where all AWS community members feel welcome and safe. This Code of Conduct explains the behavior we expect from AWS community members at interactive AWS events and programs, and across AWS blogs, online forums, and social media platforms. All attendees must comply with the AWS Code of Conduct.

### How are you keeping participants safe at re:Invent?

Your safety and security are important to us. Additional details about security measures and behavior expectations for re:Invent attendees may be found in the AWS Event Terms and Conditions and AWS Code of Conduct. All attendees at re:Invent commit to the AWS Event Terms and Conditions and AWS Code of Conduct as part of the registration process.
