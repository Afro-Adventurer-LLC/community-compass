## AWS Certification

### Will certification exams be offered at re:Invent?

Certification exams will not be offered onsite at re:Invent; however, all in-person re:Invent registrants will receive a 50% discount for any AWS Certification exam. You can access your voucher and see additional details in your attendee portal.

### Who is eligible for the 50% discount voucher toward an AWS Certification exam?

Anyone who registers to attend AWS re:Invent in person is eligible to receive a 50% discount voucher that can be applied towards an AWS Certification exam of your choice. Terms and conditions apply.

### If I registered to attend AWS re:Invent virtually, am I eligible for the discount exam voucher?

No, only those who register to attend re:Invent in person are eligible to receive a 50% discount exam voucher.

### How and when will I receive the 50% discount exam voucher?

After you register to attend re:Invent in person, you can access your voucher code in your re:Invent Attendee Portal.

### How do I use the 50% discount exam voucher?

Your exam appointment can be for any day before January 31, 2027. Offer limited to one exam voucher per individual. Offer cannot be combined with any other offers.

### When does my 50% discount exam voucher expire?

January 31, 2027, 11:59 PM (PDT).

### How can I get verified onsite to get into certification-exclusive areas?

Visit the Verify my AWS Certification desk at the Venetian on Sunday and Monday or the AWS Certification Lounge at Caesars Forum from Sunday to Friday. You can also pre-verify online directly in your re:Invent Attendee Portal.
