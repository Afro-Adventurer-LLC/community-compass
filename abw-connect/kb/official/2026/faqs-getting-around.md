## Getting Around

### Will there be transportation provided between re:Invent venues?

Yes. Shuttles travel between properties and run continuously during conference hours. Badge holders can also take the Las Vegas Monorail for free by showing their badges to the at the entrance. Transportation details will be provided in the fall.

### Do I need to be registered to ride re:Invent shuttles?

Yes. You must have a valid re:Invent badge to ride the shuttles.

### Are you offering accessible transportation?

Yes. Accessible transportation will be available during transportation hours. Attendees can make arrangements at least one hour prior to their pickup. The phone number will be available prior to the event. Please visit our accessibility services page for more information.
