## Hotels & Accommodations

### Are there discounted hotel rates for attendees?

Yes. You will be able to access exclusive discounted rates at our select hotels after completing registration through your attendee portal. The discount will be automatically reflected in the rates; no code is needed. Discounted rates are available as inventory allows through November 5, 2026.

### How do I book a hotel for re:Invent?

After completing registration, select Book or manage hotel, or log back in to your attendee portal to make a reservation. Hotels fill quickly, so we recommend making your reservation early. Sleeping room blocks close November 5, 2026.

### Will a deposit be charged to my credit card for the hotel reservation?

Yes. You are required to provide a credit card when making a hotel reservation. A one-night deposit will be charged to your card directly by the hotel prior to the event.

### When will I receive a confirmation of my hotel reservation?

After registering, you will receive a registration confirmation email followed by a separate email with your hotel acknowledgement number. Note that this is an acknowledgement, not a final hotel confirmation. You can verify your hotel booking at any time in your attendee portal. Official hotel confirmation emails will be sent directly by the hotel between November 5-13. Be sure to check your junk/spam folder if you haven't received one by then.

### How do I make changes to my reservation?

You can make changes to your hotel reservation through your attendee portal by selecting Book or manage hotel until early November. To request availability at other hotels after you've made a reservation, email customer support at awsreinvent-support@amazon.com.

### What is the cancellation policy for the hotel reservation?

All reservations made through the re:Invent block follow each hotel's cancellation policies. Please contact the hotel directly to inquire about their cancellation policy.

### I am under 21. Can I still stay at a re:Invent campus hotel?

Due to gaming regulations, you must be at least 21 years old to make a reservation at all re:Invent campus hotels, excluding Treasure Island. If you are 18 to 20 years old, you can stay at any hotel if you are checking in with someone who is 21 or over. Please reach out to awsreinvent-support@amazon.com with questions.
