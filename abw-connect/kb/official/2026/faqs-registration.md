## Registration & Passes

### How much does it cost to attend re:Invent?

Full conference passes include access to keynotes, breakout sessions, hands-on labs, and the broader event experience, including re:Play: the official re:Invent party. An add-on pass is available for re:Play-only entry. Full conference:

- Full price: $2,499
- Bulk purchase price: Save 10% when purchasing 10 or more passes in a single transaction. Terms and conditions apply.
- re:Play guest pass: $350

### Is there an age requirement to attend re:Invent?

Yes. All attendees must be at least 18 years of age by the day they pick up their conference badge.

### How do I purchase bulk discount tickets?

During registration, you'll see the option to purchase group passes. If you plan to attend the event, you'll be able to apply a pass to yourself after you complete the purchase.

Once you add 10 or more tickets to your cart, the 10% discount will automatically be applied. No discount code is needed.

Upon completing the purchase, your group codes will be available in the attendee portal under the pass management tab. Provide the registration code to each person attending, and they will apply the code when registering to remove the cost of the conference pass. Each person must register themselves through their own account.

### How can I purchase a re:Play guest pass?

During registration, select the re:Play guest pass, or choose the option to purchase additional passes from your attendee portal after you've completed registration. The purchase will generate a registration code that you will provide to your guest, which they will use to register themselves. Your guest will need to have a badge in order to attend re:Play. If you are already registered, you will not need to purchase a re:Play pass for yourself; entry is included in your general event registration.

### Do I need to be registered to attend the conference?

Yes. You need to be registered for re:Invent in order to attend any event sessions or functions. A valid conference badge and lanyard are required when in the designated event spaces.

### Are there day passes or Expo-only passes available?

No. A full conference pass is required to attend re:Invent.

### Where can I access my receipt?

Resend your receipt and confirmation email through the pass management tab in your attendee portal.

### What is your cancellation policy for conference registration?

If AWS receives your cancellation notice by Oct. 16 2026, you will be issued a full refund of your registration fees via the original payment method. If AWS receives your cancellation notice between Oct. 17, 2026, and Nov. 23, 2026, AWS will issue you a 50% refund of your registration fees via the original payment method. If AWS receives your cancellation notice after Nov. 23, 2026, you will not be eligible for a refund. Event registrations may be transferred to another participant upon approval by AWS and at its sole discretion. To request a transfer of your event registration, contact us at awsreinvent-support@amazon.com.

### I can no longer attend. How do I transfer my event registration to someone else?

Contact us at awsreinvent-support@amazon.com to transfer your registration. Transfers are approved by AWS at its sole discretion. Note that we are not able to transfer hotel or seat reservations.

### How do I update my registration information?

Profile information can be updated through your attendee portal under the Event profile tab. To make any other changes, email customer support at awsreinvent-support@amazon.com. Note that email addresses cannot be changed in your account after registration is complete.
