## Sessions & Content

### What type of content will be available in person at re:Invent?

Sessions at re:Invent cover every format and depth: workshops and labs where you build alongside AWS engineers in groups as small as 10 people, chalk talks where architects diagram solutions in real time, code talks with live demos, lightning talks, bootcamps, breakout sessions, and keynotes. 70% of session formats are interactive and practitioner-led.

### What happened to re:Inforce?

For 2026, re:Inforce is part of re:Invent. Security content runs all week, with a security focus day on Thursday, Dec. 3 at the Wynn for security practitioners to tackle advanced content and challenges together.

### When can I browse sessions and activities?

The re:Invent 2026 event catalog is now live. You can browse sessions, labs, workshops, and other activities, and mark favorites to start planning your schedule. More content will be added leading up to the conference.

### Will bootcamps be offered at re:Invent?

Bootcamps will be included in reserved seating for in-person attendees at no additional cost.

### In what venues will content be available?

Content will be delivered across multiple venues including Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, MGM Grand, The Venetian, and Wynn.

### Can I attend sessions at multiple venues?

Yes. Please note the venue where your session will take place and allow for additional travel time between venues.

### Will content be available on demand?

Yes. The keynotes and breakout sessions will be available on demand through the website after they are presented at re:Invent.

## Reserved Seating

### Will there be reserved seating?

Yes. Reserved seating will open October 6. Registered attendees can start favoriting sessions in the event catalog.

### What is reserved seating?

Reserved seating allows in-person attendees to secure a seat in a session in advance. Once reserved seating is open, attendees registered for in-person re:Invent can reserve seats in participating session types through the catalog by signing in to their attendee portal or by signing in to the AWS Events mobile app.

### What content types are included in reserved seating?

Bootcamps, technical sessions, chalk talks, code talks, exam prep, gamified learning, select labs, and workshops all include reserved seating. Attendees do not need to reserve a seat for keynotes or lecture style-breakout sessions. If a session or activity is not included in reserved seating, it will be noted with "No reserved seating available."

### When does reserved seating open?

Reserved seating is now open. Sign in to your attendee portal or the AWS Events app, navigate to the event catalog, and reserve your seats for the sessions you don't want to miss. Reserved seats are non-transferrable.

### If I reserve a seat, is my spot guaranteed?

No. You must arrive at the room at least ten minutes prior to the session start time or your seat will be released to walk-up attendees and you will be asked to join the back of the walk-up line.

### What happens if I can't reserve a seat in the session I want to attend?

Look for repeated instances of sessions (indicated with -R in the session ID) or continue checking back for availability in that session. You may also join the walk-up queue onsite.

### What is the difference between favoriting a session and reserving a seat?

Favoriting a catalog entry adds sessions and activities you're interested in to your favorites list. You can favorite multiple sessions or activities that occur at the same time as you plan out your schedule for re:Invent. For session types that are included in reserved seating, you must take the additional step of reserving that session to secure your seat. You may not reserve seats in multiple sessions that occur at the same time.

### I favorited a session. Does that mean I have reserved a seat?

No. Favoriting a session helps you better plan your time at re:Invent, but it does not reserve a seat. For session types that are included in reserved seating, you must take the additional step of reserving that session to secure your seat.
