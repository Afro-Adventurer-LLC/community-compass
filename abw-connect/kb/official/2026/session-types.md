# re:Invent 2026 session types and learning formats

- Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/sessions/how-youll-learn/
- Section: Session types
- Event year: 2026
- Source type: official
- Retrieved: 2026-11-14

## Session formats

re:Invent 2026 groups its learning formats by how you take part:

- Watch and learn: breakout sessions (expert-led talks on new services, architecture patterns and customer stories) and lightning talks (short expert talks).
- Work alongside experts: chalk talks (interactive architecture discussions with AWS engineers where you can ask questions), code talks (code along with AWS engineers and practitioners) and builders' sessions (hands-on building with AWS engineers and practitioners).
- Build it yourself: workshops (deploy and test in live environments with AWS experts on hand), labs, and gamified learning.
- Prepare for certification: exam prep sessions (interactive study with subject matter experts) and bootcamps.

## Session levels

Sessions are rated 100 to 500: 100 Foundational, 200 Intermediate, 300 Advanced, 400 Expert, 500 Distinguished. Choose a level by how well you know the topic, not by your job title.

## In numbers

More than 2,200 sessions, about 70% of them interactive; more than 150 launch sessions; more than 3,000 speakers, including 80+ principal-level engineers, architects and scientists. Registered attendees get 50% off an AWS Certification exam.
